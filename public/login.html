Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers for employees

1. How do I reset my password?

Go to Settings → Change Password section. Enter your current password and set a new one. Password must be at least 8 characters long.

Note: If you've forgotten your password, contact IT support at it-support@trimitha.co.in or call +1 (555) 123-4567.

2. How do I apply for leave?

Navigate to the Attendance tab and click "Request Leave". Select your leave dates, choose the type of leave (sick, casual, etc.), and submit. Your manager will receive a notification for approval.

Tip: Apply at least 3 working days in advance for planned leaves.

3. When will I receive my salary?

Salaries are processed on the last working day of each month. You can download your payslip from the Payroll section by the 5th of the following month.

Note: Contact payroll@trimitha.co.in if you don't receive your salary by the 7th.

4. How do I update my personal information?

Basic information can be updated in your Employee Details section. For sensitive changes (name, date of birth, bank details), contact HR department for verification.

Contact: hr@trimitha.co.in or +1 (555) 987-6543

5. What should I do if I can't login?

First, ensure you're using the correct Employee ID and password. If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link or contact IT support immediately.

Emergency: 24/7 IT Support: +1 (555) 123-4567

6. How do I access company documents?

All shared documents are available in the Documents tab. You can browse folders, search for files, and download what you need. Some documents may require specific permissions.

7. What is Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?

2FA adds an extra layer of security to your account. After entering your password, you'll need to enter a code from your authenticator app. This is mandatory for all employees.

Setup: You'll be guided through setup on your first login.

8. How do I report a technical issue?

Use the "New Ticket" button in the Help & Support section to submit an IT ticket. Describe the issue in detail and attach screenshots if possible.

Response Time: Initial response within 2-4 hours during business hours.

9. Where can I find company policies?

The Employee Handbook is available for download in the Help & Support section. It contains all company policies, code of conduct, and guidelines.

10. What if I need emergency assistance?

For urgent matters outside business hours, use the emergency contact numbers provided in the Help & Support section. IT support is available 24/7 for critical issues.

Emergency Contacts: Available in the Help & Support tab.
